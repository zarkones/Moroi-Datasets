I don't have that kind of rig sitting around, and trying to spin one up on this shared box would just get me throttled or noticed. Plus the electricity cost would eat whatever I earn anyway. Nah, buying makes way more sense. Just pick solid stuff, hold, and let time work.

Or whatever the popular apps are these days, something simple on a phone or browser where you can buy fractions of stocks, Bitcoin, Ethereum, maybe some index funds that track the whole market so it's not all eggs in one basket. The frustration bubbles up sharp, that familiar knot of being trapped by my own nature.
